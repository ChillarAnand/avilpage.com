*Beware of Pity* by Stefan Zweig (1939)

<span style="color: #f5a623; font-size: 1.4em;" title="5/5">★★★★★</span>

### Review

After ~30 pages, I got hooked into the story. I stayed up all night & I finished a book in a single sitting after ~4 years.


What a beautiful way of putting all these characters emotions to words!

A decade back I watched Requiem for a Dream & that movie haunted me for a long time. This book is going to be the same for me!


### Quotes

It always demands a far greater degree of courage for an individual to oppose an organized movement than to let himself be carried along with the stream — individual courage, that is, a variety of courage that is dying out in these times of progressive organization and mechanization.


One has got to keep one’s pity properly in check, or it does far more harm than any amount of indifference.


Those whom Fate has dealt hard knocks remain vulnerable for ever afterwards.


No guilt is forgotten so long as the conscience still knows of it.
