I have been [using RayCast](/2025/06/raycast-replaced-10-macos-apps.html) from ~4 years. I built 2 extensions, ~20 custom scripts, contributed to existing extensions. 

I raised support tickets related to automating RayCast exports & settings. Unfortunately, there was no good resolution.

### FOSS Alternatives

When looking at open source alternatives I stumbled on [Vicinae](https://github.com/vicinaehq/vicinae), [RustCast](https://github.com/MystikoLab/rustcast) long back but they were not drop in replacements.

![raycast-drop-in-replacement](/images/tinycast-raycast-open-source-alt.png)

Recently [TinyCast](https://github.com/abue-ammar/tinycast) came out and I am using it as a primary launcher on one of the Mac. It is working well as a drop-in replacement so far without any major issues.

It is just ~6MB app and consumes only a fraction of CPU/Memory compared to other tools.

### Software as Moat

With AI, it is getting easy to build & customise software. There is a growing trend where users are shifting towards open source alternatives(since I am in FOSS space, I might be biased).

If you think software only is a moat, it is time to re-think again!
